The [hidden] contract

The booking engine opens and closes the travellers dropdown, the calendar, and the whole expansion card by toggling the hidden attribute — not a class. A component that sets display would override the UA default and pin those open. 03-base.css reasserts it with !important. The box below must stay invisible.
